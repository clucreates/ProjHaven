Draft your squad. Break the Hollow King.

Pick 5 heroes — at least one Tank, one Damage, one Support — then face The Hollow King through all four of his phases. He fights in rotating stances — Bulwark armor, Reaver fury, Oracle healing — scouted below so your draft is strategy, not guessing. His adds have jobs: Mends heal him, Wards shield him, so target priority matters. Tap a picked hero to choose its loadout — one ability variant, one ult variant. In battle, heroes fight on their own, but focus fire and ultimates are yours: tap any enemy to converge the squad on it, and fire charged ults into the King's Exposed windows, when he takes +50% damage. Out-draft the boss, then out-play him.

Squad orders — autoplay policy

Focus fire is yours: tap any enemy to set the squad's focus — every single-target attack and ult converges on it until it dies or you tap it again (or the gold chip) to release. Unfocused, the squad works the priority list: Mend adds → Ward adds → Thralls → the King. Abilities fire on cooldown when their trigger is met; tanks taunt the King onto themselves; supports heal the most wounded ally. Combo: consecutive hits by different heroes build the combo meter — cash it in for bonus damage on the next squad strike (+5% per count, up to +100%), ideally into an Exposed window. Ultimates charge as heroes deal and take damage — with Auto-ult on, a hero fires theirs during Exposed windows or once the King is below half health; turn Auto-ult off and every ult waits for your tap.
