Draft your squad. Climb the tower.

Pick 5 heroes — at least one Tank, one Damage, one Support — then climb 10 floors: Swarm, Sanctum, the Hollow Herald, a damage Race, and the Hollow King — then the tower hardens. Elite adds with affixes (Thorns, Hasted, Bulwarked, Vampiric), a reinforced Sanctum+, the Twin Heralds fighting as one, a tighter Race, and the King's True Form. Clear Floor 10 and the climb goes ENDLESS: procedurally generated floors, enemies scaling +12% per floor, boons after every floor — the run ends only when the squad wipes. After each floor you draft 1 of 3 boons to patch your squad — and the tower sometimes pays more: a gold Bounty Draft lets you pick 2 of 3, and a rare Jackpot Draft grants all 3. Drafts are dealt against your squad's live state, so the options on offer are ones that can actually help. Permadeath is absolute: a wipe on any floor ends the run — full restart, no checkpoints. Fallen heroes stay down unless a resurrection ult or boon brings them back. From the Floor 6 draft onward, drafts can offer a gold Tier II elite form — one hero transformed for the rest of the run: a new ability, a changed silhouette, a gold role icon. Elite is permanent for the run, never stacks, and a hero revived mid-floor returns with their elite suppressed until that floor is cleared. Tap a picked hero for its loadout (some supports can pick a resurrection ult). In battle, focus fire, combo cash-ins and ultimates are yours — fire ults into Exposed windows (+50% damage).

Squad orders — autoplay policy

Focus fire is yours: tap any enemy to set the squad's focus — every single-target attack and ult converges on it until it dies or you tap it again (or the gold chip) to release. Unfocused, the squad works the priority list: Oracle adds → Mend adds → Ward adds → Thralls → the boss. Abilities fire on cooldown when their trigger is met; tanks taunt the King onto themselves; supports heal the most wounded ally. Combo: consecutive hits by different heroes build the combo meter — cash it in for bonus damage on the next squad strike (+5% per count, up to +100%), ideally into an Exposed window. Ultimates charge as heroes deal and take damage — with Auto-ult on, a hero fires theirs during Exposed windows or once the King is below half health; turn Auto-ult off and every ult waits for your tap.
